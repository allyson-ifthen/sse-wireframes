4Introduction
Ohio Natural Gas is a natural gas supplier licensed by the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio (PUCO) to serve Ohio residents and businesses. As a deregulated supplier, we offer fixed and variable rate plans so customers can choose their commodity rate rather than accepting the default utility price. Ohio Natural Gas also offers Greener Life® — a natural gas plan that makes your home’s gas usage carbon neutral through certified offset purchases. Ohio Natural Gas is a brand of SouthStar Energy Services, LLC, a subsidiary of Southern Company (NYSE: SO).
