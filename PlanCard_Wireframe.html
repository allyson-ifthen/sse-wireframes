Plan Card — unified component spec

Single source of truth for the plan card used on Residential Plans, Commercial Plans, Promo Pages, and the new IES Fixed Bill page. Replaces three legacy class systems (.plan-card, .offer-card, .comm-plan-card) with one component that exposes variants and states.

✓PC-D10 Promo is a state, not a separate class. Same component instance renders on the Promo Page, on Plans Listing pinned-promo (see §5.b below), and on any standard listing where an intro-rate plan appears — no duplicate authoring.
✓walkthrough rev Promo state covers two scenarios: (a) True promos with promo_code + expiration, gated via the Promo Page LDC-required flow (Promo SAVE25 example); (b) Intro-rate plans with was-price + savings claim but no code or expiration, surfaced on the standard Plans Listing alongside other plans (Variable with Discount example). Visual treatment is the same — was-price, Slot K disclosure, "promo" or "intro" badge — but placement and lifecycle differ.
✓walkthrough rev Promo state surfaces two additions in fixed order: was-price (top-right of rate row, struck-through) and expiration line (⏱ glyph above description). Promo code carried by the header badge ("Promo SAVE25"). Expiration format: MM/DD/YYYY at HH:MM PM/AM ET — date alone is insufficient for promotions that legally end at a specific moment.
⚑regulatory Was-price MUST reference the actually-published standard rate for the same plan term and market — not an inflated "MSRP" anchor. Strikethrough is a strong ecommerce signal but it implies a real, verifiable savings claim. CMS authoring must pull was-price from the canonical standard-rate field, not from a separate authored value. Variable with Discount: was-price must equal the published variable rate at the moment the intro rate is offered.
✓Slot K Required disclosure line lives below the bullets, above the expand toggle. Anchor points to a page-level Promo Disclosure block (bottom of Promo Page · parallel position on Plans Listing pinned-promo). Full legal text never lives on the card.
⚑combinatorics Promo state × Most Popular badge — NOT allowed. Promos are time-limited offers; "Most Popular" is a volume-derived signal for the standing plan catalog. The two would compete for badge real estate and confuse the customer about what's actually being promoted.
✓combinatorics Promo state × Greener Life variant — allowed but unobserved today. Greener Life is new in these markets, no Greener Life promo has been run. If introduced, the visual treatment is: Greener Life green border + green rate color (variant wins visual hierarchy), badges read "● Fixed Rate · 🌱 Greener Life · Promo [CODE]," strikethrough was-price colored to match the green rate, Slot K disclosure covers BOTH the savings claim AND the environmental-disclosure pointer. Multi-rate × promo remains forbidden per PC-D4.

⚑Disclosure copy for promo plans — content/legal team to confirm minimum required language before publishing. Where it lands: page-level Promo Disclosure block (Slot K anchor target).
