How It Works
How Choosing a Supplier Works

Natural gas deregulation in OhioPennsylvaniaMichiganIllinois means you can choose your supplier instead of defaulting to your utility's rate — here's what that means in practice.

1
Choose Your Supplier
In deregulated states like OhioPennsylvaniaMichiganIllinois, you're not required to accept the rate your utility sets. Ohio Natural GasPennsylvania EnergyGrand Rapids EnergyIllinois Energy is one of the licensed suppliers you can choose from.
2
Your Utility Still Delivers
Choosing Ohio Natural GasPennsylvania EnergyGrand Rapids EnergyIllinois Energy as your supplier doesn't change how your gas gets to your home. Your utility continues to maintain the pipes, handle emergencies, and deliver the gas. That part never changes.
3
One Bill, One Account
The Ohio Natural GasPennsylvania EnergyGrand Rapids Energy supply charge appears as a line item on your regular utility bill. One bill, same payment process.
3
Two Bills — One Supplier, One Utility
Because of how Illinois billing works, Illinois Energy sends you a separate supply bill. Your utility bill covers delivery; the Illinois Energy bill covers your commodity rate.

Not sure who your utility is? Your utility's name is on every gas bill — or you can look it up on the PUCOPA PUCMPSCICC website.

✓ Three-step explanation addresses the #1 barrier to enrollment: confusion about what changes when you switch suppliers ⛔ IL billing model is material and required — two-bill explanation is not optional for IL
