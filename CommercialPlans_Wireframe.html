Frequently Asked Questions

What commercial natural gas plans are available for my business?
[Brand] offers fixed-rate and variable-rate plans for small to mid-size businesses. Fixed plans lock in your supply rate for the contract term; variable plans adjust monthly with the market. Plan options and pricing vary by utility — select your gas utility above to see what's available for your address. [Note: Final plan catalog pending Lee Jackson confirmation — Open Q #1.]
Is there an early termination fee for commercial plans?
Fixed-rate plans include an early termination fee (ETF) of [$ TBD]/month for each remaining month on your contract if you cancel early. Variable plans have no ETF — you can cancel anytime. [ETF amount pending Lee Jackson confirmation — Open Q #5.]
How do I enroll my business online?
Select your utility above, choose the plan that fits your business, and click "Start Enrollment." You'll complete enrollment online through our secure system. Have your account number and service address ready. Most small commercial enrollments take about 10 minutes.
How is my commercial gas rate determined?
Fixed-rate plans lock in your supply rate for the contract term — your rate won't change even if market prices rise. Variable-rate plans adjust each month based on market conditions and can go up or down. All rates are supply rates only; your utility's delivery charges appear separately on your bill.
My business has high usage or multiple locations. What are my options?
If your business uses high volumes of natural gas or operates across multiple locations, our commercial energy team can work with you on a custom arrangement. Click "Contact a Rep" above or use the banner below to connect with a specialist — no obligation.
