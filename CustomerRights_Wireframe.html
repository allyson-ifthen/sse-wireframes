1 Your Right to Choose Your Supplier

Ohio Natural GasPennsylvania EnergyGrand Rapids EnergyIllinois EnergyIllinois Energy Solutions is licensed by the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio (PUCO)Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission (PA PUC)Michigan Public Service Commission (MPSC)Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC)Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC) to sell natural gas supply to customers in a state with energy choice. Energy choice means you get to pick which company supplies the actual natural gas commodity that reaches your home — it does not change who delivers it.

Your local distribution company (LDC) — Columbia Gas of OhioUGI UtilitiesConsumers EnergyNicor Gas / Peoples GasNicor Gas / Peoples Gas — continues to deliver your gas through the same pipeline system, read your meter, handle billing for delivery charges, and respond to emergencies no matter which supplier you choose. That relationship does not change when you enroll with, or leave, Ohio Natural GasPennsylvania EnergyGrand Rapids EnergyIllinois EnergyIllinois Energy Solutions.

You are never required to stay with us. You can switch to a different licensed supplier, or return to your utility's default supply service, at any time — subject to the terms of your current contract. See our Legal & Compliance page for Early Termination Fee details if you're still in a fixed-term contract.

2 Your Right to Cancel (Rescission Window)

Pennsylvania gives you 3 business days from the date you receive your enrollment confirmation to cancel your enrollment without penalty, as required by the PA PUC. To exercise this right, contact SouthStar customer service using the phone number in the footer of this page.

3 What to Do If You Were Switched Without Your Consent

If you believe you were enrolled with Ohio Natural GasPennsylvania EnergyGrand Rapids EnergyIllinois EnergyIllinois Energy Solutions without your permission, contact SouthStar customer service right away using the phone number in the footer of this page. Please have your account number and service address ready if you have them.

We will look into what happened. If we confirm your enrollment wasn't authorized, we will work with your utility to reverse it and restore your prior service arrangement. This does not happen instantly — utility validation runs on a nightly batch, so a reversal can take a few business days to take effect on your account.

If your concern isn't resolved after contacting SouthStar, you have the right to escalate a complaint to PUCOthe PA PUCthe MPSCthe Illinois ICCthe Illinois ICC — see How to File a Complaint, below.

4 How to File a Complaint

If you're not able to resolve a concern about your service directly with SouthStar, you can file a complaint with the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio (PUCO)the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission (PA PUC)the Michigan Public Service Commission (MPSC)the Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC)the Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC), the state agency responsible for oversight of natural gas suppliers in your area.
