1 What Happens When Your Fixed Term Ends

Your fixed-rate contract term with Grand Rapids Energy has ended. At this point, Grand Rapids Energy's process is to roll your account onto a variable-rate plan.

⚑
Open Item #2: Whether this rollover is automatic (you've already been moved, this page is informing you after the fact) or something you actively choose to do is not confirmed. Copy below is deliberately written so either framing can be dropped in without a structural rewrite — do not read the wording below as confirming either direction.

[Rollover framing placeholder — pending Open Item #2:] Your plan is now billed at Grand Rapids Energy's current variable rate. Unlike your fixed-rate plan, the variable rate can move up or down periodically based on market conditions rather than staying locked for a set term.

Your bill is measured in Mcf (thousand cubic feet), not therms — this is a Grand Rapids Energy / Michigan-specific unit and differs from how usage is billed in SouthStar's other markets.

3 How the Variable Rate Can Change

Your variable rate moves based on wholesale market conditions for natural gas, rather than staying fixed for a contract term. It can go up or down from one billing cycle to the next.
