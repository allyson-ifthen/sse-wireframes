What Makes Fixed Bill Different
With the Fixed Bill plan, your monthly natural gas charge is set once, based on your home's usage history, and stays the same for the length of your plan term — regardless of how cold the winter gets or how much gas you use in any single month. It's reviewed and reset at renewal, not adjusted mid-term.
For a lot of households, the hardest part of a gas bill isn't the total cost over a year — it's not knowing what a single month's bill will be. A surprise cold snap can turn into a surprise bill. Fixed Bill removes that uncertainty by giving you one number to plan around, every month, all term long.

Your Utility Relationship Doesn't Change
Illinois Energy Solutions is not your gas utility. Your utility — Nicor Gas, Peoples Gas, or North Shore Gas, depending on your service territory — still delivers your gas, reads your meter, and responds to any gas emergency. Illinois Energy Solutions supplies the gas itself and bills you for it under the Fixed Bill structure; nothing about who shows up at your home changes.
