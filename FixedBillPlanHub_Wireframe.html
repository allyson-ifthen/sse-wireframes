What Fixed Bill Is
Fixed Bill gives you one flat monthly charge — set from your home's natural gas usage history, not a live meter read or a per-therm rate — so a cold snap or a mild month never changes what you owe.
1
One Flat Number, Set From Usage History
Your monthly charge is set using your home's natural gas usage history — not a live meter read, and not a per-therm calculation applied to whatever you use that month.
2
Same Bill, Every Month, All Term
A colder-than-usual January or a mild March doesn't change what you owe — your charge stays the same regardless of weather swings or short-term usage changes within your term.
3
Reviewed and Reset at Renewal
As your term ends, we calculate a new charge from your more recent usage and let you know your options before your current term expires. Nothing happens automatically without you being notified first.

Is the Fixed Bill Plan Right for Me?
You Might Like Fixed Bill If
  • You'd rather know your exact gas charge every month than have it swing with the weather.
  • You've been caught off guard by a big bill spike in a past winter and want to avoid that surprise again.
  • You're comfortable paying slightly more some months and slightly less others, in exchange for one flat number all term long.
When Fixed Bill May Cost More
  • During a mild month, you may pay more on Fixed Bill than you would have on a usage-based rate that month — that's the tradeoff for one predictable number, not a flaw in the plan.
  • Fixed Bill is built to guarantee predictability, not the lowest possible total cost every season.
  • If minimizing total spend matters more to you than predictability, a usage-based plan may fit better.

Good to Know
How Pricing Compares
Fixed Bill doesn't have a per-therm rate to compare directly — your charge is set from your own usage history, not tracked to the utility's monthly rate. For reference, Nicor Gas' current supply cost rate is $0.33 per therm (through 9/30/2026).
Your Utility Doesn't Change
Nicor Gas still delivers your gas, reads your meter, and responds to gas emergencies. Illinois Energy Solutions supplies the gas and bills you under the Fixed Bill structure — nothing about who shows up at your home changes.
Licensed & Independent
✓ ICC Licensed Supplier. Illinois Energy Solutions is not affiliated with Nicor Gas.
