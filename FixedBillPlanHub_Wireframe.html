What Makes Fixed Bill Different
With the Fixed Bill plan, your monthly natural gas charge is set once, based on your home's usage history, and stays the same for the length of your plan term — regardless of how cold the winter gets or how much gas you use in any single month. It's reviewed and reset at renewal, not adjusted mid-term.
For a lot of households, the hardest part of a gas bill isn't the total cost over a year — it's not knowing what a single month's bill will be. A surprise cold snap can turn into a surprise bill. Fixed Bill removes that uncertainty by giving you one number to plan around, every month, all term long.
How It Works
1
One Flat Number, Set From Usage History
Your monthly charge is set using your home's natural gas usage history — not a live meter read, and not a per-therm calculation applied to whatever you use that month.
2
Same Bill, Every Month, All Term
A colder-than-usual January or a mild March doesn't change what you owe — your charge stays the same regardless of weather swings or short-term usage changes within your term.
3
Reviewed and Reset at Renewal
As your term ends, we calculate a new charge from your more recent usage and let you know your options before your current term expires. Nothing happens automatically without you being notified first.

Is the Fixed Bill Plan Right for Me?
You might like Fixed Bill if:
  • You'd rather know your exact gas charge every month than have it swing with the weather.
  • You've been caught off guard by a big bill spike in a past winter and want to avoid that surprise again.
  • You're comfortable paying slightly more some months and slightly less others, in exchange for one flat number all term long.
Dev: condensed from NP-8's 4-item list to the 3 tightest signals. Descriptive framing only ("you might like Fixed Bill if...") — no quantified savings claims, per Universal Guidance §1.
When Fixed Bill May Cost More
It's also important to understand when Fixed Bill may not be the cheapest option. Because your Fixed Bill charge is set in advance from your usage history, there will be some months — especially during a mild season, when your actual usage would have been fairly low on a standard variable rate — where you end up paying more under Fixed Bill than you would have paid on a usage-based rate that month.
That's not a flaw in the plan; it's the direct tradeoff of the plan's whole purpose. Fixed Bill exists to give you one flat, predictable number every month, not to guarantee you the lowest possible total cost in every season. If your priority is minimizing your total spend no matter what, a usage-based plan may serve that goal better than Fixed Bill will.

Understanding Bill Predictability
Illinois requires natural gas suppliers to disclose the utility's current supply cost rate — you'll find that comparison on our Residential Plans page. Fixed Bill doesn't have a per-therm rate to compare directly: your charge is set from your own usage history, not tracked to the utility's monthly rate. That's not a signal Fixed Bill costs more or less in any given month — just a different pricing model, kept in one current, regularly-updated place rather than duplicated here.

Your Utility Relationship Doesn't Change
Illinois Energy Solutions is not your gas utility. Your utility — Nicor Gas — still delivers your gas, reads your meter, and responds to any gas emergency. Illinois Energy Solutions supplies the gas itself and bills you for it under the Fixed Bill structure; nothing about who shows up at your home changes.
