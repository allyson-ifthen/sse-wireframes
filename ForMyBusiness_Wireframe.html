How Commercial Supply Works
How Commercial Natural Gas Supply Choice Works
1
Choose Your Supplier
In deregulated markets, businesses can choose their natural gas supplier instead of defaulting to their utility's supply rate. Ohio Natural GasGrand Rapids EnergyIllinois Energy is a PUCOMPSCICC-certified commercial supplier in OhioMichiganIllinois.
2
Your Utility Still Delivers
Your local utility — Columbia Gas of Ohio, Dominion Energy Ohio, Duke Energy Ohio, Enbridge Gas OhioConsumers Energy, DTE EnergyNicor Gas, Peoples Gas, North Shore Gas, Ameren Illinois — continues to deliver natural gas to your business through the same pipes. The utility handles emergencies, meter reading, and physical delivery. Ohio Natural GasGrand Rapids EnergyIllinois Energy handles your supply rate.
3
One Bill, Two Line Items
In most cases, your supply charge appears as a line item on your existing utility bill. You'll see two charges: utility delivery and Ohio Natural Gas supply.
3
One Bill, Two Line Items
In most cases, your supply charge appears as a line item on your existing utility bill. You'll see two charges: utility delivery and Grand Rapids Energy supply.
3
Two Separate Bills
Illinois commercial customers receive a separate bill from Illinois Energy for the supply portion of their natural gas. Your utility bill covers delivery; the Illinois Energy bill covers supply. You'll manage two bills.

Switching is handled online for most commercial accounts. There's no interruption to your service — your gas keeps flowing throughout.

IL separate-billing is material for commercial B2B — must be stated clearly, not deferred to enrollment. Requires legal review (B2B-09). Utility names per market listed in Step 2 — ONG · GRE · IL confirmed
