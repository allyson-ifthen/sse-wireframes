What Is
Greener Life
What Is Greener Life®?

Greener Life® is a natural gas plan. When you enroll, Ohio Natural GasGrand Rapids Energy calculates the greenhouse gas emissions produced when your home burns natural gas — then purchases and retires certified carbon offsets equal to that amount. The result: your natural gas usage is carbon neutral.

“Carbon neutral” on a Greener Life® plan means that the greenhouse gas emissions from the combustion of your natural gas — the carbon dioxide released when gas burns in your furnace, water heater, or range — are balanced by certified carbon offsets retired on your behalf. This covers combustion emissions at the point of use.

The greenhouse gas emissions offset by Greener Life® are those associated with the combustion of natural gas at the point of consumption. They do not include lifecycle emissions that occur during extraction, production, or delivery of the gas to your home. This is a common boundary in combustion offset programs. We name it here so you can make an informed choice.

[PLACEHOLDER: certification standard — working assumption Green-e® Climate (same as GNG); client must confirm ONG/GRE use same standard before publishing with named standard (GL-01)]. Certified offsets meet independently verified standards for additionality, permanence, and measurability.

“Retiring” a carbon offset means permanently removing it from the market — it is recorded in an official, independent registry as used on your behalf and cannot be sold or claimed again. This prevents double-counting and ensures the offset credit applies to your usage only.

Additionality
The emissions reduction would not have happened without the offset funding — the project would not exist otherwise.
Permanence
The emissions reduction is lasting — a forest planted as an offset, for example, must remain a forest, not be cleared in five years.
Measurability
The amount of carbon avoided or removed is calculated to a verified, consistent standard — not estimated or assumed.

Does switching to Greener Life® change my utility service?
No. Your utility — [PLACEHOLDER: ONG utility names — GL-03] — still delivers your natural gas and handles delivery charges, billing disputes, and emergencies. Greener Life® changes your commodity rate supplier. The gas in your pipes is the same gas. Is my natural gas actually different on a Greener Life® plan?
No. Natural gas is a commodity — the gas that arrives at your home is not individually tracked to your supplier. What changes is the accounting: Ohio Natural GasGrand Rapids Energy retires certified carbon offsets equal to the emissions your household produces when burning that gas. The offset is what makes your usage carbon neutral, not the gas itself.
What emissions does Greener Life® cover?
Greener Life® covers the greenhouse gas emissions produced when natural gas burns in your home — your furnace, water heater, stove, and any other gas appliance. It does not cover lifecycle emissions from extracting, processing, or transporting gas to your home. This is a common boundary in combustion offset programs.
What if I move or cancel my plan?
If you move or cancel, your plan ends on the date of cancellation. Fixed-rate plans may carry an early termination charge (ETC) — check your enrollment agreement for terms. [PLACEHOLDER: offset retirement timing — confirm against actual Greener Life® plan agreement (GL-05)]
How do I know the offsets are real and not just a claim?
Certified offsets are recorded in an official, independent registry — each offset has a unique ID and is marked as retired when used on your behalf. This prevents double-counting: an offset retired for your Greener Life® plan cannot be sold or claimed by anyone else.
