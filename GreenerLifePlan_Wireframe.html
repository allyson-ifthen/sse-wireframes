What Is
Greener Life
What Is Greener Life®?

Greener Life® is a natural gas plan. When you enroll, Ohio Natural GasGrand Rapids Energy calculates the greenhouse gas emissions produced when your home burns natural gas — then purchases and retires certified carbon offsets equal to that amount. "Carbon neutral" means the combustion emissions from your home's gas usage are balanced by certified offsets retired on your behalf.

This covers combustion emissions at the point of use — not lifecycle emissions from the extraction, production, or delivery of gas to your home. What does carbon neutral gas actually mean? →

Common Questions About Greener Life®
Does switching to Greener Life® change my utility service?
No. Your utility — [PLACEHOLDER: ONG utility names — GL-03] — still delivers your natural gas and handles delivery charges, billing disputes, and emergencies. Greener Life® changes your commodity rate supplier. The gas in your pipes is the same gas. No. Your utility — [PLACEHOLDER: GRE utility names — GL-04] — still delivers your natural gas and handles delivery charges, billing disputes, and emergencies. Greener Life® changes your commodity rate supplier. The gas in your pipes is the same gas.
Is my natural gas actually different on a Greener Life® plan?
No. Natural gas is a commodity — the gas that arrives at your home is not individually tracked to your supplier. What changes is the accounting: Ohio Natural GasGrand Rapids Energy retires certified carbon offsets equal to the emissions your household produces when burning that gas. The offset is what makes your usage carbon neutral, not the gas itself.
What emissions does Greener Life® cover?
Greener Life® covers the greenhouse gas emissions produced when natural gas burns in your home — your furnace, water heater, stove, and any other gas appliance. It does not cover lifecycle emissions from extracting, processing, or transporting gas to your home. This is a common boundary in combustion offset programs.
What if I move or cancel my plan?
If you move or cancel, your plan ends on the date of cancellation. Fixed-rate plans may carry an early termination charge (ETC) — check your enrollment agreement for terms. [PLACEHOLDER: offset retirement timing — confirm against actual Greener Life® plan agreement (GL-05)]
How do I know the offsets are real and not just a claim?
