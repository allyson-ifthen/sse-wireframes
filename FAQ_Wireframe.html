What do I need to enroll? FAQ Schema

Is there a fee to cancel? FAQ Schema
+
Fixed-rate plans have an early termination fee (ETF) if cancelled before the contract end date. Ohio Natural Gas fixed plans carry a $12.50/month ETF for each remaining month on the contract. Pennsylvania Energy fixed plans carry a $10.00/month ETF for each remaining month on the contract. Grand Rapids Energy fixed plans carry a $20.00/month ETF for each remaining month on the contract. Illinois Energy ETF amount — confirm with client. Illinois Energy Solutions ETF amount — confirm with client. Variable-rate plans and plans within their renewal window can be cancelled without an ETF.
→ Understanding your contract terms
Why do I need a verification call to complete enrollment? IL only FAQ Schema
+
Illinois requires 100% sales verification for all natural gas supplier switches. After you complete enrollment online, our team will call you (or you'll receive a recorded verification) to confirm your switch. This is an Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC) requirement, not a sales call.
What is the 3-day rescission right? PA only FAQ Schema
+
Pennsylvania law gives you the right to cancel your enrollment within 3 business days without penalty. This is your right under the PA Public Utility Commission. You can cancel by calling us or by written notice within 3 business days of enrollment.
Billing & Payments
Who do I pay my gas bill to? FAQ Schema
+
You continue to pay your bill directly to your utility (Columbia Gas or Dominion Energy Ohio). Ohio Natural Gas does not issue a separate bill — your gas supply charge from us appears as a line item on your existing utility bill. You continue to pay your bill directly to your utility (UGI, Peoples, or PECO). Pennsylvania Energy appears as a supplier line item on your existing utility bill. You continue to pay your bill directly to your utility (DTE or Consumers Energy). Grand Rapids Energy appears as a supplier line item on your existing utility bill. Illinois Energy issues a separate bill for your gas supply. You will receive two bills: one from your utility for delivery, and one from Illinois Energy for supply. Illinois Energy Solutions issues a separate bill (Fixed Bill). You will receive two bills: one from your utility for delivery, and one from IES for your fixed monthly supply charge.
Why do I see two charges on my bill? FAQ Schema
+
Your gas bill has two components: (1) Delivery charge — paid to your utility for maintaining the pipes and infrastructure. (2) Supply charge — the cost of the natural gas itself, billed at your plan rate. When you enroll with us, we supply your gas at the rate you chose. Your utility still delivers it.

My Plan & Rates
What's the difference between a fixed and variable rate? FAQ Schema
+
A fixed rate locks in a set price per therm (or MCF) for the duration of your contract — typically 6–24 months. Your rate won't change even if market prices rise. A variable rate follows the monthly market index and can go up or down. Variable rates have no contract term or early termination fee.
→ Natural Gas Basics: Fixed vs. Variable Rates → View current plans
What is the Greener Life plan? ONG + GRE FAQ Schema
+
Greener Life is a separate plan type (not an add-on) that offsets the carbon emissions associated with your natural gas usage through certified renewable natural gas (RNG) or carbon offsets. You pay a slightly higher rate in exchange for a carbon-neutral energy supply. It is available as a fixed or variable plan.
