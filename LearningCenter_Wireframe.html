✦ Key Takeaways
A fixed-rate natural gas plan locks in a set price per therm for the duration of your contract, protecting you from market price increases. A variable-rate plan fluctuates monthly based on market conditions. Fixed-rate plans typically include an Early Termination Fee if cancelled before the contract end date.

What Is a Fixed Rate?

A fixed-rate natural gas plan locks in a set price per therm for the length of your contract. Your supply rate will not change even if the wholesale price of gas increases.

What Is a Variable Rate?

A variable-rate plan adjusts monthly based on market conditions. Your rate may be lower than a fixed rate some months — but it can also be significantly higher during periods of high demand or supply disruption.

Can I switch from a variable rate to a fixed rate at any time? +
Yes, you can enroll in a fixed-rate plan at any time. If you're already on a fixed-rate plan and want to change to a different plan, Early Termination Fee rules may apply depending on your contract state.
Is a fixed-rate plan guaranteed to be cheaper than my utility's rate? +
No — a fixed-rate plan is about stability, not guaranteed savings. During periods of low market prices your utility's rate may be lower. The benefit of a fixed rate is predictability and protection against price spikes.
What happens when my fixed-rate contract ends? +
[Answer content TBD — covers auto-renewal, rollover to variable, renewal notice timeline. Confirm per market.]
Does switching suppliers affect my gas delivery or safety service? +
No. Your local utility always remains responsible for delivering gas to your home and for all emergency responses. Switching your gas supply does not affect delivery, pressure, or safety in any way. The utility handles delivery; we handle supply.
