ENR-198 (2026-08-25) — production stepper navigation/validation rule. `ENR-56` left every chip above freely clickable at all times, scoped as "reviewer navigation freedom; production validation separate" (IL's `ENRIL-109` carries the identical carve-out) — the wireframe's chips stay that way intentionally, for reviewer convenience. The real production rule, decided but not built here: (1) a customer cannot advance to a step without completing the prior step(s) — no forward skip-ahead; (2) once a step is completed, the customer can navigate back to it via the stepper; (3) editing a completed step re-locks that step and every step after it — the customer re-confirms (taps Continue on) each remaining step in order on their way back to Review, without re-entering data. This is how a customer corrects a CustomerLookup-verified field (e.g. the now-locked "Service & account" section, `ENR-197`) — go back to Step 1 via the stepper and resubmit, which forces re-verification. Functionally equivalent to IL's "start over" framing (`ENRIL-303`) without a literal restart. Also closes a document-accuracy gap: without this, a customer could edit data after already checking Accept Contract's consent boxes, submitting a contract that no longer matches what they affirmed. See `ENR-199` for the related edit-return-banner fix. Applies to both steppers above (New Customer chip-1–chip-4; Existing Customer chip-pf3–chip-4-ex).
