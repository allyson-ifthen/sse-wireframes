Refer a Friend FAQs
When will I get my reward?
Your $25 bill credit posts after your friend's enrollment passes utility validation and their service start date — typically 1–2 business days after they enroll, sometimes longer depending on the local utility.
What happens if my friend doesn't enroll?
Nothing — your referral code stays active and you can share it with someone else. There's no penalty for unredeemed referrals.
Can I refer myself with a second account?
No — self-referrals are not eligible. Each referral must be a new customer at a different service address.
How long does my code stay active?
Your code is active as long as you remain an active customer. If you cancel service, the code stops accepting new referrals but any pending referrals already in flight will still count.
Is there a limit on how many people I can refer?
Reward caps and program limits may apply — see the program terms below for the full eligibility rules.
