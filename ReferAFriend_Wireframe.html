How It Works
Three steps, start to finish.
1
Share your link
Log in to My Account to get your unique referral link or code, then share it with a friend by email, text, or social media.
2
Your friend enrolls
Your friend enters your referral code during enrollment — it's the "Referral code" field near the end of the sign-up form, and it's optional so it never blocks their enrollment.
3
You both get rewarded
Once your friend's enrollment is confirmed by their utility, you'll both receive your reward. Confirmation can take a little longer than sign-up itself.

Common Questions
Does my friend need to be a new customer?
Yes. Your friend needs to be a new customer who isn't already enrolled with us — the program rewards bringing in someone new, not an existing account switching plans.
When do I get my reward?
After your friend's enrollment is confirmed by their utility — not right when they sign up. Utility confirmation runs on a nightly batch, so it can take a little longer than the enrollment itself. (Exact fulfillment timeline: see Open Item #2 above.)
Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?
There may be a limit on how many rewards you can earn — but the current program cap for 2026 hasn't been confirmed yet (see Open Item #1 above), so we can't state a specific number here until it is.
What if my friend doesn't complete enrollment?
No reward is issued unless your friend's enrollment goes through and their utility confirms the switch. If they don't finish enrolling, or the switch can't be validated, that referral doesn't earn a reward.
How do I check my referral status?
Log in to My Account to see your referral status and reward history. Log in to manage your referrals →
