Is the Fixed Bill Plan Right for Me?
A genuine self-assessment, not a sales pitch — including when Fixed Bill might cost you more, not just when it helps.
4Content Body
Good Fit Signals
You might like Fixed Bill if:
  • You'd rather know your exact gas charge every month than have it swing with the weather.
  • You manage your household budget around fixed, predictable expenses.
  • You've been caught off guard by a big bill spike in a past winter and want to avoid that surprise again.
  • You're comfortable paying slightly more some months and slightly less others, in exchange for one flat number all term long.
Dev: descriptive framing only ("you might like Fixed Bill if...") — no quantified savings claims, per Universal Guidance §1 (this is a billing-model choice, not a savings product).
When Fixed Bill May Cost More
It's also important to understand when Fixed Bill may not be the cheapest option. Because your Fixed Bill charge is set in advance from your usage history, there will be some months — especially during a mild season, when your actual usage would have been fairly low on a standard variable rate — where you end up paying more under Fixed Bill than you would have paid on a usage-based rate that month.
That's not a flaw in the plan; it's the direct tradeoff of the plan's whole purpose. Fixed Bill exists to give you one flat, predictable number every month, not to guarantee you the lowest possible total cost in every season. If your priority is minimizing your total spend no matter what, a usage-based plan may serve that goal better than Fixed Bill will.

How to Decide
There's no calculator that can tell you which plan will save you more this year — nobody knows what next winter will actually look like. So the real question is what matters more to you: if knowing your exact gas bill every month, with no surprises, matters more than chasing the lowest possible total over the year, Fixed Bill is built for exactly that. If you're comfortable with your bill moving up and down some months in exchange for a shot at a lower total, a usage-based plan might suit you better.

Common Questions
Can I switch off Fixed Bill later?
Yes — like any plan, you can shop for a different plan or supplier once your term is up, or contact us sooner to discuss your options.
Does Fixed Bill protect me from a bad winter?
Yes, in the sense that your monthly charge won't spike because of one especially cold month — that's exactly the kind of swing Fixed Bill is designed to remove.
Is Fixed Bill more expensive than my utility's rate?
It depends on the season and your usage pattern — not a blanket yes or no. In some months a usage-based rate would cost you less; in others, more. Fixed Bill trades that month-to-month variation for one flat number all term. See "When Fixed Bill May Cost More" above for the honest version of this tradeoff.
What if my usage changes a lot year to year?
Your Fixed Bill charge is reviewed and reset at renewal based on more recent usage, so a meaningful change in how much gas you use will generally show up in your next term's charge rather than mid-term.
