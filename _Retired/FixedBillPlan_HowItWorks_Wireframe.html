How the Fixed Bill Plan Works
A plain explanation of how your monthly charge is set and why it stays the same all term — no sales pitch, just the mechanics.
4Content Body
How Your Monthly Charge Is Set
When you enroll in the Fixed Bill plan, Illinois Energy Solutions sets your monthly charge using the natural gas usage history at your service address — not a live meter read, and not a per-therm calculation applied to whatever you use that month. We look at how much gas your home has typically used across a full year and set one flat number that becomes your monthly supply charge for the length of your plan term.
That number is set once, at enrollment, and reviewed again at renewal — it isn't recalculated every billing cycle. A colder-than-usual January or a mild March doesn't change what you owe that month; the whole point of Fixed Bill is that your charge stays the same regardless of weather swings or short-term usage changes within your plan term.

Usage History & New Customers
If you're new to your address and don't have a usage history with us yet, Illinois Energy Solutions still needs a way to set your first Fixed Bill charge — but the exact method for quoting a customer with no usage history isn't finalized, so we aren't describing it here until it is. If you're switching from your utility's standard variable rate or from another supplier, your existing usage history at that address is generally what's used to calculate your Fixed Bill charge going forward.

Your Utility Relationship Doesn't Change
Enrolling in the Fixed Bill plan doesn't change who delivers your gas. Your utility — Nicor Gas — still owns the pipes, reads your meter, delivers your gas, and responds to any gas emergency. Illinois Energy Solutions sets and bills the supply charge for the gas itself under the Fixed Bill structure; we never take over delivery, meter reading, or emergency response.

What Happens at Renewal
Your Fixed Bill term has a defined length. As it approaches its end, Illinois Energy Solutions calculates a new charge based on your more recent usage history and lets you know your options before your current term expires. You can choose to renew under the new Fixed Bill charge, or shop for a different plan — with us or another supplier — instead. Either way, nothing happens automatically without you being notified first.

Common Questions
Does my bill ever change during my term?
No — your Fixed Bill charge stays the same every month during your plan term, no matter how much gas you use or how the weather changes.
What if I use more gas than usual one month?
Your monthly charge stays the same either way. Fixed Bill is built so a high-usage month and a low-usage month cost you the same amount.
Is this the same as budget billing from my utility?
Not quite. Utility budget billing spreads out a rate that still changes month to month, so your utility may adjust your budgeted amount over time to catch up with actual costs. Fixed Bill is a flat charge set by Illinois Energy Solutions for your whole term — it isn't adjusted mid-term the way budget billing can be.
What happens if I move?
Your Fixed Bill plan is tied to your service address, so moving typically means starting a new plan at your new address. Contact us when you know your move date and we'll help you transition.
