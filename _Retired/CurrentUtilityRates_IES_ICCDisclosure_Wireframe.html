Understanding Bill Predictability with the Fixed Bill Plan
How Illinois's rate-disclosure requirement works, and why the comparison looks different for a Fixed Bill customer.
4Content Body
Key Takeaways
Illinois requires natural gas suppliers to disclose the utility's current supply cost rate — you'll find that comparison on our Residential Plans page. Illinois Energy Solutions' Fixed Bill charge works differently: it's one flat monthly amount based on your own usage history, not a per-therm rate, so it isn't a direct side-by-side comparison.
Dev: no dollar figures and no comparative/superlative language in this box, per the brief's Voice & Tone guardrail. Final wording requires legal sign-off (Open Item #4).
Why Your Bill Works Differently Here
Illinois Energy Solutions is a natural gas supplier licensed by the Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC). As part of that regulation, we're required to show you your utility's current supply cost rate before presenting our own plan information — you'll see that comparison on our Residential Plans page.
That comparison works a little differently for Fixed Bill customers than it does for a standard rate plan. Most suppliers' plans charge a rate per therm, so showing the utility's per-therm rate next to a supplier's per-therm rate is a straightforward comparison. Fixed Bill doesn't have a per-therm rate at all — your charge is set from your own usage history at enrollment, which is exactly what makes it predictable from month to month.
Dev: does not use "ICC-approved" — the ICC requires the disclosure ordering, it does not certify the pricing, per the brief's callout (same rule as the parallel IE article, NP-3).
Why You'll See the Utility's Rate on the Plans Page
Illinois Energy Solutions is regulated by the Illinois Commerce Commission, and that regulation requires your current utility's supply cost rate — Nicor Gas — to be shown before our own charge information. You'll see that comparison on our Residential Plans page rather than repeated here: the utility's rate resets monthly, so a static table on this page would go out of date the same day it's published.
Showing it in one place, kept current, protects you from seeing two different numbers in two different spots that quietly drift apart. Think of the ordering requirement as consumer protection, not a sales comparison — it exists so you always have the utility's number in view, whichever plan you choose.

What Predictability Means for Your Bill
As a Fixed Bill customer, your monthly charge is set from your own usage history rather than tracking the utility's live per-therm rate month to month. That means there's no month where your bill moves because the broader gas market moved — your number was already set when you enrolled.
The utility rate shown on our Residential Plans page satisfies Illinois's disclosure requirement; it isn't a signal that Fixed Bill costs more or less than that rate in any given month, just that it's a different pricing model. If you want the honest picture of when Fixed Bill can cost more than a usage-based rate, see Is the Fixed Bill Plan Right for Me?, which addresses that directly.

Common Questions
Why doesn't this page show the current utility rate directly?
Because that rate changes monthly and varies by utility, we keep one current, regularly-updated version of it on our Residential Plans page instead of a second copy here that could go stale.
Is IES's Fixed Bill charge always higher or lower than paying the utility rate directly?
Neither, consistently — it depends on the season and your usage pattern. Fixed Bill trades that month-to-month variation for one flat number; see Is the Fixed Bill Plan Right for Me? for the full honest breakdown.
Where can I see the utility's current rate?
On our Residential Plans page, where Illinois law requires us to display it before our own plan information.
