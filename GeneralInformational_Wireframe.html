About Ohio Natural Gas Pennsylvania Energy Grand Rapids Energy Illinois Energy Illinois Energy Solutions
Ohio Natural Gas is a competitive natural gas supplier licensed to operate in Ohio. We provide residential and commercial customers with fixed-rate and variable-rate natural gas supply plans — including the Greener Life® carbon-neutral option. Pennsylvania Energy is a competitive natural gas supplier licensed by the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission (PA PUC). We serve residential and commercial customers across participating utility service areas in Pennsylvania. Grand Rapids Energy is a competitive natural gas supplier serving residential and commercial customers in Michigan. We’re licensed by the Michigan Public Service Commission (MPSC). Illinois Energy is a competitive natural gas supplier licensed by the Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC). We serve residential and commercial customers across Nicor Gas and Peoples Gas service territories. Illinois Energy Solutions is a fixed bill natural gas supplier serving residential customers in Illinois, licensed by the Illinois Commerce Commission (ICC).
