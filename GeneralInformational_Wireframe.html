Addressing Common Questions
🤔
Common Concern
Is a third-party supplier actually safe? What happens if the company goes away?
Your local utility always remains responsible for delivering gas to your home and responding to emergencies — that never changes when you switch suppliers. Your utility distribution service is separate from gas supply. We source the gas; they deliver it. Even if your supplier changes, your service does not get interrupted.
💵
Common Concern
Am I really saving money, or is this just a gimmick?
A fixed-rate plan isn’t primarily about saving money — it’s about predictability and protection from market spikes. When gas prices rise, your rate stays the same. The value is in stability and peace of mind, not a guaranteed bill reduction.
📋
IES Specific
What is a Fixed Bill plan, and how is it different from a fixed rate?
A fixed-rate plan locks in your price per therm but your bill still varies based on how much gas you use. A Fixed Bill plan locks in your total monthly charge — so the amount you pay is the same every month regardless of usage or price changes, up to your plan’s usage threshold.
