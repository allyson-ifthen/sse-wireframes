5How It Works
How Commercial Natural Gas Supply Works
1
You Choose Your Supplier
In deregulated markets, businesses can choose their natural gas supplier instead of defaulting to their utility’s supply rate. Ohio Natural Gas is a licensed supplier certified by PUCO to offer commercial plans in Ohio. Grand Rapids Energy is a licensed supplier certified by MPSC to offer commercial plans in Michigan. Illinois Energy is a licensed supplier certified by the ICC to offer commercial plans in Illinois.
2
Your Utility Still Delivers
Your local utility continues to deliver natural gas to your business through the same pipes. The utility handles emergencies, meter reading, and physical delivery. Ohio Natural Gas handles your supply rate. Grand Rapids Energy handles your supply rate. Illinois Energy handles your supply rate.
3
One Bill, Two Line Items
In most cases, your supply charge appears as a line item on your existing utility bill. You’ll see two charges: utility delivery and Ohio Natural Gas Grand Rapids Energy supply.
3
Two Bills — One Supplier, One Utility
Illinois commercial customers receive a separate bill from Illinois Energy for the supply portion of their natural gas. Your utility bill covers delivery. Illinois Energy’s bill covers supply. You’ll manage two bills. Important for AP teams: budget for two line items. [PLACEHOLDER: IL commercial billing copy requires legal review (OI #10)]
Enrolling is handled online. There’s no interruption to your service — your gas keeps flowing throughout the switch.

6Plan Options
Commercial Plan Options
See available rates for your business on the Commercial Plans page — select your utility to view current options.
Fixed Rate
Lock in your rate for a defined contract term.
Fixed-rate plans make energy cost predictable across your budget cycle. Your supply rate stays the same for the duration of your contract regardless of market movement. An Early Termination Charge (ETC) applies if the plan is cancelled before the contract end date. Billed per Mcf. Billed per therm. Billed per therm.
Variable Rate
Move with the market. No long-term commitment.
Your commodity rate adjusts monthly with market conditions. Variable-rate plans offer flexibility without a long-term commitment — no ETC. Rate changes are communicated before each billing period. Best suited for businesses with flexible budgets or that anticipate market price decreases.
