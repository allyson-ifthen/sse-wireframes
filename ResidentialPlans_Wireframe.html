Common Questions About Natural Gas Plans

FAQ sourced from Ohio call center data (581 pricing/plan calls/month) and ONG query pattern analysis. FAQ schema (JSON-LD) applied to all items.
What's the difference between a fixed rate and a variable rate? ▼
A fixed rate locks in your price per therm (or Mcf) for the length of your contract — your supply rate won't change even if market prices rise. A variable rate adjusts monthly based on market conditions and can go up or down. Fixed-rate plans typically include an Early Termination Fee if cancelled before the contract end date; variable plans generally do not.
What is an Early Termination Fee (ETF)? ▼
An Early Termination Fee is a charge that applies if you cancel a fixed-rate plan before your contract end date. The fee is calculated per remaining month on your contract. ETF amounts vary by market — see your plan details for the specific fee. Variable rate plans have no early termination fee.
Will my utility or gas service change when I switch? ▼
No. Your local utility company continues to deliver gas to your home and respond to any emergencies — that never changes. When you switch to [market brand], you're choosing a new supplier for the commodity portion of your bill. Your pipes, your meter, and your utility's phone number stay exactly the same.
How long does it take to switch? Will my service be interrupted? ▼
Enrollment is done entirely online. Your switch typically takes effect at your next meter read — there is no service interruption at any point. You'll continue to receive one bill from your utility, which will reflect your new supply rate.
